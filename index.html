Research & Academic Projects

My research interests focus on transportation systems, accessibility, urban development and the application of analytical methods to understand planning challenges.

Undergraduate Thesis

Assessing Passenger Satisfaction and Perceived Accessibility of Metro Rail Transit (MRT) Users in Dhaka: A Study of Travel Behavior and Service Quality.

Study Area: Dhaka MRT Line-6

Research Design: Passenger intercept survey involving 400 respondents across 16 MRT stations.

Methodology: Survey data collection using KoboToolbox, data processing in Microsoft Excel, accessibility assessment and quantitative analysis using PLS-SEM in SmartPLS.

Research Focus: Passenger satisfaction, perceived accessibility, service quality and travel behavior.
